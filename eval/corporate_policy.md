# Global Corporate Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy
**Document ID:** POL-CORP-TE-2026-V2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Audience:** All Full-Time, Part-Time Employees and Contractors  

---

## 1. Airfare & Flight Travel

### §1.1 Economy Class Standard
All domestic and short-haul international flights (under 6 hours in duration) must be booked in standard Economy / Coach class. Business Class or Premium Economy travel is strictly prohibited for flights under 6 hours unless an executive medical exemption is on file with People Operations.

### §1.2 14-Day Advance Booking Requirement
All flight reservations must be booked at least 14 days in advance of the departure date to take advantage of corporate fares. Flights booked within 14 days of departure require written Director-level approval attached to the claim; otherwise, the claim will be flagged for review.

### §1.3 International Travel Authorization
All international travel requires prior written authorization from the Department Vice President at least 30 days prior to departure. Any international trip submitted without pre-authorization documentation will be flagged for management review.

### §1.4 Seat Selection & Cabin Upgrades
Standard seat selection fees are reimbursable. Paid cabin upgrades (e.g., extra legroom seats over $50, Economy Plus, First Class upgrades, or auction bidding upgrades) are personal expenses and will not be reimbursed by the company.

---

## 2. Ground Transportation & Commuting

### §2.1 Rideshare, Taxi, and Public Transit
Taxis, rideshares (Uber, Lyft standard / comfort), and public transit incurred directly for business travel between airports, lodging, and client/work sites are reimbursable up to a maximum of $50.00 per single one-way trip. Rideshare trips exceeding $50.00 require a business reason detailing distance or surge pricing. Luxury rideshare tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are strictly non-reimbursable.

### §2.2 Personal Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement
Employees using personal vehicles for approved business travel may claim mileage at the standard corporate rate of $0.67 per mile. Daily commuting between an employee's residence and their designated primary office is not eligible for reimbursement under any circumstance.

### §2.3 Rental Car Class & Fuel Policy
When rental cars are required, reservations must be made for Compact or Intermediate/Mid-Size vehicles only. SUV, Luxury, or Sports car rentals are non-reimbursable without prior VP approval. Prepaid fuel options and optional rental insurance (covered by corporate credit card policy) will be rejected.

### §2.4 Airport Long-Term Parking
Airport parking is reimbursable only for economy / long-term parking lots up to a maximum daily cap of $30.00 per day. Valet parking and premium short-term terminal parking charges are non-reimbursable.

---

## 3. Meals & Business Dining

### §3.1 Daily Total Meal Per Diem Cap
The maximum daily reimbursement for all individual meals combined during overnight business travel is $75.00 USD per day (inclusive of taxes and standard tip up to 20%). Daily meal expenses exceeding $75.00 will be flagged, and any portion exceeding 1.5x ($112.50) will be rejected.

### §3.2 Single Meal Individual Limits
Individual meals during single-day travel or non-overnight trips are capped as follows: Breakfast ($15.00), Lunch ($20.00), and Dinner ($40.00). Individual meal expenses within these thresholds are approved automatically.

### §3.3 Client Entertainment & Business Dining
Meals hosted with external clients, partners, or prospective accounts are reimbursable up to a maximum of $100.00 USD per person (including host). All client entertainment claims must include an itemized receipt and a complete list of attendees with their company affiliations and business discussion topic.

### §3.4 Group Dining Itemization & Split Bills
When multiple employees dine together, the most senior employee present must pay the bill and submit the claim with names of all attending employees listed. Splitting a single group bill across multiple employee expense claims to circumvent individual limits is strictly prohibited.

---

## 4. Alcohol & Prohibited Personal Expenses

### §4.1 Strict Alcohol Prohibition (Zero Tolerance)
The company maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding alcohol reimbursement. Any claim containing beer, wine, liquor, cocktails, or any alcoholic beverage charge will be automatically rejected. In mixed receipts, the alcohol portion must be deducted before submission; if submitted in full, the entire line item or claim will be rejected.

### §4.2 Personal Entertainment & In-Room Amenities
Hotel pay-per-view movies, fitness/spa services, hotel mini-bar snacks/beverages, in-flight streaming/entertainment fees, and personal sightseeing tours are non-reimbursable personal expenses.

### §4.3 Traffic Violations, Parking Tickets, and Fines
Any moving violations, speeding tickets, parking fines, toll violations, or vehicle towing fees incurred during business travel are the sole legal and financial responsibility of the employee and will be rejected with zero exceptions.

### §4.4 Spouse & Companion Travel Expenses
Travel, lodging, and meal expenses for spouses, family members, or traveling companions who are not active company employees on official business are strictly non-reimbursable.

---

## 5. Hotel & Lodging Standards

### §5.1 Standard Room Rate Cap
Hotel accommodations must be booked in standard single-occupancy rooms. Nightly room rates are capped at $250.00/night (before local taxes) in designated Tier 1 metropolitan cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo, Zurich) and $180.00/night in all other locations. Rates exceeding these caps by up to 20% due to peak city events will be flagged for review; rates exceeding 20% without prior approval will be rejected.

### §5.2 Hotel Incidentals, Internet & Laundry
High-speed in-room internet access is reimbursable up to $15.00/day if not provided complimentary by the hotel. Laundry and dry cleaning services are reimbursable only for business trips lasting five (5) consecutive business days or longer.

### §5.3 Direct Billing & Cancellation Penalties
Hotel cancellations must be made within the hotel's free cancellation window. No-show penalties and late cancellation fees will be rejected unless caused by an unavoidable business emergency documented in writing.

---

## 6. Remote Office Supplies, Hardware & Software

### §6.1 Home Office & Peripheral Hardware
Remote employees may claim essential home office peripherals (e.g., monitor, mouse, keyboard, ergonomic chair) up to an annual limit of $200.00 USD per calendar year. All hardware purchases require prior written approval from the direct manager prior to purchasing.

### §6.2 Software Subscriptions & SaaS Tools
Software subscriptions, cloud services, and AI developer tools required for business workflows require approval from the IT Department and are capped at $50.00/month per individual tool. Unapproved software charges will be flagged.

### §6.3 Expedited Courier & Shipping Services
Standard shipping for business-related documents and packages is fully reimbursable. Expedited overnight or courier shipping exceeding $40.00 requires written justification stating the business urgency.
